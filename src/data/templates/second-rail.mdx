## About this template

Second Rail is a modern clothing store front end built around a unique thrift-shop inspired browsing experience. Instead of a classic product grid, garments hang on a horizontal zip-line style clothing rail. Shoppers scroll left and right through the hanging clothes in side view, then select an item to see clean front and back views.

Once a piece is chosen, users can move to a fully functional cart where they change size, color and quantity. The overall aesthetic is deliberately clean and minimal — soft neutrals, generous white space and refined typography — so the interface feels calm and considered rather than noisy.

The template includes the complete Next.js source with the horizontal rail interaction, product detail views and cart flow, ready for customization or connection to a real backend.
