## About this template

Shop Store is a marketplace front end for independent brands and considered goods. The home page leads with a row of featured brands, then breaks into category sections, each with its own tile layout, so the page feels edited rather than like a product dump.

Shoppers get dedicated pages for search, saved items and order tracking, plus a profile area and an app download banner. The footer covers the usual shopper, merchant and legal pages, so the template reads as a complete marketplace.

This entry points at the public demo only, for testing the catalog, the live preview and the checkout flow.
